About

As a Hardware Engineer, I focus on designing, testing, and improving electronic hardware systems that are reliable, efficient, and fit for purpose. My work can include circuit design, component selection, prototyping, troubleshooting, and hardware validation, with attention to performance, safety, and manufacturability. I enjoy translating technical requirements into practical solutions and collaborating across engineering teams to solve hardware challenges. Through a methodical, hands-on approach, I aim to help develop dependable products and support their smooth progression from concept to implementation.
